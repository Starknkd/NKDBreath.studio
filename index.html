What is this

A first-of-its-kind physiology experience

Stark NKD is a London-based human-performance brand combining psychology, breathing science, and immersive technology. The NKD Breath Lab is a pop-up space where students can see — in real time — what their breathing actually looks like under pressure.

This isn't a breathing class. It's data. Your data. Most people have never had access to it before.

The founder

Meet Anna

I'm Anna Stark — a human-performance researcher, breath specialist, and founder of Stark NKD. My work combines psychology, respiratory science, and emerging technology to help people understand how their bodies react under pressure.

Before building Stark NKD, I spent nearly two decades in senior leadership roles. During Covid, I became fascinated by the science of the nervous system and began exploring technologies that could help people regulate stress more effectively. That path led me to retrain as a breathing behaviour analyst and to pursue a Master's degree in human performance in extreme environments.

I created the NKD Breath Lab to give students a fun, safe, hands-on way to explore their own physiology — not through lectures or theory, but through immersive experience.
